ResearchSpace Semantic Web Platform: A Living Knowledge Process

ResearchSpace is not just a digital platform — it is a dynamic process of sense-making, designed to evolve alongside the inquiries and communities it serves. Rooted in material-processual principles and supported by CIDOC CRM, ResearchSpace facilitates the transformation of historical evidence into contextualised knowledge in a native Linked Data format.

From a processual perspective, ResearchSpace operates through the following core phases:

  • Expert Authoring: Primary materials—oral histories, images, texts, and data—are introduced not as static records, but as evolving patterns of human experience.
  • Annotation and Interpretation: Contributors link entities, express uncertainty, and build layered meaning with dialectic potential.
  • Semantic Connections: Using CIDOC CRM, relationships are made explicit, expressing how and why things relate across time and context.
  • Reconfiguration: Interpretations evolve as projects grow. ResearchSpace embraces this recursive, dynamic process.
  • Engagement and Circulation: Knowledge is re-expressed in public contexts—exhibitions, storytelling, dialogue—and remains open for reuse.

This approach emphasizes that knowledge is not a product, but a practice. ResearchSpace acts as a temporal scaffold, adapting to collaboration, change, and discovery.
